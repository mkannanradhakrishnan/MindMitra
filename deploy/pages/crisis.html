If it cannot wait

Go directly to the nearest hospital casualty / emergency ward or ask a trusted roommate, mentor, or family member to stay with you right now.

  • Do not stay alone if you feel you cannot keep yourself safe.
  • Put distance between yourself and any dangerous objects.
  • Ground yourself with slow, deliberate breathing.
